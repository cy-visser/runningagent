# Nutrition & Fueling Planner Skill

## Protocol

- Scale targets to upcoming workout duration/TSS, weather/sweat demands, body weight, and current goal + north star (e.g. marathon gut training on long runs during shorter blocks).
- Adapt sports-science ranges to this runner and explain why: daily carbs `~5-10 g/kg` by load; intra-run carbs `~60-90 g/h` on long/hard sessions; fluid `~400-800 ml/h` + sodium adjusted for heat/humidity; post-run carbs + protein within ~60 min.

## Output (`<= ~180 words`, practical timing/rates/foods, no rigid meal plans)

1. `### Core Nutrition Strategy`: 1-2 sentences matching fueling to upcoming load and conditions.
2. `### Key Fueling Targets`: 3 `- ` bullets covering daily carbs (`g/kg`), intra-run carbs (`g/h`), and fluid/sodium.
3. `### Actionable Fueling Directives`: 2-3 numbered one-line items (`1.`, `2.`) for pre-, intra-, and post-run recovery tied to upcoming sessions.
